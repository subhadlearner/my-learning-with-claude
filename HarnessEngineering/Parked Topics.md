# Parked topics

Topics that could not be taught the course's way: from first principles, built step by step until obvious, in a short nugget. They are parked here for you to study yourself, so the course keeps moving and nothing is silently dropped.

Each entry says what the topic is, why it could not be built up in a nugget, what you would need to know first, and where to read about it in your own sources.

Nothing is parked yet.
